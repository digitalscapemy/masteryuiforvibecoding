PRO NOTE

"Propagation" is really caching. Resolvers keep old answers until the TTL runs out, and a nameserver change waits on the TLD's TTL.

Check what the internet sees: dig sitiscakes.com NS +short or nslookup.

A CNAME cannot sit on the root in standard DNS. Cloudflare allows it through CNAME flattening.

Nameservers: who holds the phonebook
