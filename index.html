05

Spacing:Padding vs Margin

Padding is inside the element. Margin is outside the element.

Padding

Inner spacing (inside the element)

Padding
Content
  • Adds space inside the element
  • Pushes the content away from the border
  • Affects the element's internal layout

Margin

Outer spacing (outside the element)

Margin
Content
  • Adds space outside the element
  • Pushes the element away from other elements
  • Does not affect the element's internal content
